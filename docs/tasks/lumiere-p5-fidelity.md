Method: the same scroll sequence on our build (local production build, fixtures, EN) and on `https://opt-lumi-re.vercel.app/`, at 1440×900 and 375×812, section by section (hero, collections, featured, philosophy, footer); screenshots are on the orphan branch `evidence/lumiere-p5` (`fidelity/cmp-d-*.png`, `cmp-m-*.png`: ours left, reference right). Every difference below is intended unless marked **DEFECT** or **OPEN**.
