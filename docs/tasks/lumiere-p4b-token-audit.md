## Navigation gold (finding A4) — the one place gold carries meaning again
Dedicated tokens in `app/lumiere/nav.css`, mirrored in `tailwind.config.ts` (`nav-gold`): `--nav-gold #C9A227` (underline, indicator, dot, eyebrow rule; text only on black), `--nav-gold-text #8A6B12` (small text on WHITE only: 5.0:1; on bone it is 4.42:1, under AA), `--nav-gold-deep #6F5510` (hover/current/eyebrow text on bone and white, 6.2–7.0:1). Applied to the header (labels, toggle, account, cart, search), the menu overlay, the account dropdown, the search overlay, the phone tab bar, the footer (headings and links) and the account side list / chips. Every pair is a ledger row (`nav: …`); the pale gold line on bone (2.1:1) is decorative and never the only cue — the text beside it changes colour too.

## Contrast rows added in P4b
Ledger total 346 pairs (307 after the navigation rows, 288 at P4). New: navigation 19 (`nav:`), content pages 20 (`ic:`), commerce pages 10 (`icm:`), account pages 9 (`iac:`). Rule kept: gold accent lines on paper are decorative (recorded, not gated) and never the only cue; small gold text on bone is the deep gold only.
